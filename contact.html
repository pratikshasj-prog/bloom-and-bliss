Frequently Asked

Delivery & Ordering FAQs

Quick answers to your most common questions about our bouquets and express delivery.

How does same-day delivery work?
Orders placed before 6:00 PM are eligible for express same-day delivery within 2 to 3 hours across metro cities. We also offer fixed-time slots and midnight delivery (11:30 PM – 12:30 AM) for birthdays and anniversaries.
Can I include a personalized message and special wrap?
Yes! Every order includes a complimentary gold-embossed message card where your custom note is handwritten with calligraphy. You can also opt for luxury satin wrap at checkout.
How do you guarantee the freshness of your flowers?
All flowers are harvested daily from our partner farms in Bangalore and Ooty. Stems are placed in hydrated floral foam or sponge pouches during transit, and each order comes with flower food nutrients to keep blooms radiant for 5+ days.
What payment methods are supported?
We support instant UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit and Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) in select pin codes.
