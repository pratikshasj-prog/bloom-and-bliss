The Bloom & Bliss Story

Turning Ephemeral Petals into Timeless Sentiment

Founded with a simple yet ambitious vision: to transform flower and gift delivery from a mundane transaction into an artisanal sensory celebration. We noticed that online gifting was dominated by wilted stems, plastic wraps, and lackluster generic chocolates.

Bloom & Bliss bridges that gap by collaborating directly with certified greenhouse growers across Bangalore, Ooty, and Himachal Pradesh. Our floral designers combine modern European floral aesthetics with heartfelt Indian hospitality.

Whether it’s a surprise bouquet delivered at midnight or a bespoke anniversary wooden frame, we treat every single order as if it were being delivered to our own loved ones.

What Sets Us Apart

Our Guiding Values

The four pillars that guide our floral design studios every day.

🌱

100% Farm-Direct Sourcing

We skip middle markets and auction delays. Our flowers are cut at the crack of dawn and hydrated in cold-chain facilities to guarantee 5+ days of lively bloom life.

🎀

Bespoke Artistry

No automated conveyer belts. Every arrangement is hand-tied by trained floral artisans using double-faced satin ribbons, frosted tissues, and signature craft paper.

♻️

Eco-Conscious Packaging

We use recyclable kraft papers, compostable plant hydration pouches, and reusable wooden trunk boxes to ensure our blooms honor nature.

🤝

Unconditional Happiness

Your delight is our commitment. If any bloom or gift arrives damaged or fails to enchant, our customer concierge provides an immediate replacement or full refund.
